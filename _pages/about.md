Hi! I am a Research Associate in Computer Vision at the University of Bristol, working on the [TORUS project](https://torus.ac.uk/about-torus/), where I develop learning-based, multimodal video systems for long-term human motion representation and analysis in real-world environments. My work focuses on building robust and privacy-aware vision pipelines that operate reliably under changing conditions and practical deployment constraints.

I completed my PhD in Computer Vision at the University of Bristol, where I developed deep learning methods for video-based analysis of human movement, with a focus on Parkinson’s disease. My PhD was supervised by [Professor Majid Mirmehdi](https://majidmirmehdi.github.io) and [Professor Alan Whone](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/alan-l-whone).

My research interests include video understanding, multimodal and vision–language learning, and developing adaptive and efficient learning systems that generalise across environments, particularly in limited or unlabeled data settings and real-world deployment scenarios.
