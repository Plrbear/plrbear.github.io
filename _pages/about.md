Hi! I'm a Research Associate in Computer Vision at the University of Bristol, where I work on the \href{ https://torus.ac.uk/about-torus/}{TORUS project}. We develop AI-based video systems to monitor human movement in home environments over extended periods, transforming raw visual data into meaningful, privacy-preserving features to support real-world clinical applications. I recently completed my PhD in Computer Vision at University of Bristol, where I focused on deep learning strategies for Parkinson’s disease assessment. My research was supervised by \href{https://majidmirmehdi.github.io}{Professor Majid Mirmehdi} and \href{https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/alan-l-whone}{Dr. Alan Whone}.

I am interested in video understanding, and fascinated by how we can make learning systems more adaptive and efficient, especially when working with limited or unlabeled data, changing environments, and practical constraints.
