Hi! I'm a Research Associate in Computer Vision at the University of Bristol, where I work on the [TORUS project](https://torus.ac.uk/about-torus/), developing AI-based video systems to monitor Parkinson's patients' movement in home environments over extended periods. I completed my PhD in Computer Vision at University of Bristol, where I focused on deep learning strategies for Parkinson's disease assessment via video data. My research was supervised by [Professor Majid Mirmehdi](https://majidmirmehdi.github.io) and [Professor Alan Whone](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/alan-l-whone).

I am interested in video understanding, and fascinated by how we can make learning systems more adaptive and efficient, particularly under limited or unlabeled data settings, changing environments, and practical constraints.
